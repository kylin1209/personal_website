About

I'm Kylie Lin, a Computer Science student at New York University pursuing a B.A. in Computer Science and Mathematics and a Master of Science in Computer Science through the bachelor's-master's track. I'm driven by the intersection of machine learning, infrastructure, and systems design, with a particular interest in building tools that turn complex data into meaningful, actionable outcomes.

My experience spans research engineering, DevOps, distributed systems, and software development. At NYU, I led research-engineering initiatives and managed workflows for a broad range of applications. At Bessemer Trust, I built and deployed a firm-wide micro-frontend to automate trading workflows and developed a custom GPT workflow that improved performance on the Investment Management database by 600%. Earlier, I worked in DevOps and software engineering at Maximus, where I focused on automation, deployment streamlining, and operational efficiency.

I enjoy working across the stack, from infrastructure and observability to full-stack product development and applied ML. My strongest interests are in engineering systems that are both technically rigorous and practically useful—especially projects that combine data, automation, and user-centered design.

I’m currently interested in software engineering, research, and machine learning opportunities where I can contribute to impactful work at the intersection of technical depth and real-world application. My experience and projects are summarized below.
